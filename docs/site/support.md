# Akakū app: help and support

## What the app does

The Akakū app lets you watch Akakū's three channels live (53, 54 and 55), listen to KAKU 88.5 FM, watch Akakū's videos, and read plain-English recaps of Maui County meetings. You can follow the issues you care about and see what is new on them. The app is free.

## Where the recaps come from

Akakū's AI reads the captions of recorded public meetings and writes short summaries in plain English. Every statement has a time stamp: tap it and you go straight to that moment in the recording, so you can check it yourself.

AI can make mistakes. The recording is the source, and a recap is not the official record of a meeting. Recaps are information, not legal advice.

## I found a mistake

Tap **Report an error**, which appears under each statement and at the bottom of every issue and recap. Add a comment if you like, and tap Send. Please do not include personal information.

## Following issues

- Open the **Meetings** tab. Your issues are listed under **Issues you follow**.
- Tap **Edit what you follow** to add or remove issues.
- New people start out following a few issues, and you can change that at any time.

## Something is not working

**A live channel will not play.** Check your internet connection and try again. If you are on cellular data, try Wi-Fi. Make sure the app is up to date. If it still does not play, write to us and tell us your phone model and which channel.

**The radio will not play.** Tap play again. KAKU 88.5 keeps playing when you lock your phone or switch to another app. If it stops, tap play to rejoin the live broadcast.

**The Videos tab is empty.** Check your connection and reopen the app. The Videos tab shows Akakū's own YouTube videos.

## Watching on a TV or speaker

Tap the AirPlay or cast icon next to the video or radio player to send it to a TV or speaker on your Wi-Fi.

## Bigger text and light or dark

On the **Live** tab, tap **Aa** for larger text and the moon or sun for dark or light mode. If anything is hard to see or use, please tell us.

## Giving to Akakū

Open the **Support** tab and tap **Give or become a member**. You can give once or become a monthly member.

## About Akakū

Akakū Maui Community Media is a nonprofit community media center. We are not part of the County of Maui and cannot help with County services. For those, visit mauicounty.gov.
