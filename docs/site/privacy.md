# Akakū app: privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

## Who we are

The Akakū app is published by Akakū Maui Community Media, operated by Maui County Community Television, Inc., 333 Dairy Road, Suite 104, Kahului, Hawaiʻi 96732. In this policy, "Akakū" and "we" mean that organization. You can reach us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## The short version

The Akakū app does not ask you to create an account. It has no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

## What stays on your phone

The app remembers a few things on your device so it works the way you left it:

- the issues you follow
- your display choices (light or dark, large type)
- whether you have dismissed a tip
- a saved copy of recent recaps, issues and videos, so the app opens quickly

We never receive this information. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## What we receive if you report an error

Under each statement, issue and recap there is a "Report an error" link. If you use it, we receive:

- what you reported (for example the sentence, issue or meeting, and the video moment it points to)
- the comment you write, if any
- your device type (iPhone or Android) and the app version

We use this only to check the item and correct it if it is wrong. Please do not put personal information in the comment. Reports are collected through a form hosted by Google (Google Forms) and delivered to Akakū by email. We do not ask for your name or email address and we do not reply unless you give us a way to.

## Other services the app connects to

When you use the app, it connects to the services below. They can see your IP address and basic device information, as any website or app you use would, and their own privacy policies apply.

- **Akakū's content.** Recaps and issues are files hosted on GitHub, and the channel schedule and preview images come from akaku.org.
- **Live television.** Channels 53, 54 and 55 stream from Akakū's video provider (Castus, delivered through Amazon CloudFront).
- **KAKU 88.5 FM.** The radio stream comes from Akakū's radio streaming provider (Securenet Systems).
- **YouTube.** Akakū's videos, their thumbnails and the video player come from YouTube, which is owned by Google. YouTube may collect information when you watch, under Google's privacy policy.
- **AirPlay and casting.** These are handled by your phone's operating system.
- **Giving.** When you tap "Give or become a member", the app opens akaku.org in a browser. Gifts are handled on our website and its payment provider. The app never sees your payment details.

## What the app does not do

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not send notifications at this time. If we add notifications or accounts later, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.

## Summaries written by AI

The meeting recaps and issue pages are written by artificial intelligence from recordings of public meetings. They can make mistakes, and they are not the official record of any meeting. Every statement links to the moment in the recording so you can check it yourself. If something is wrong, please use "Report an error."

Public meeting recordings include officials and members of the public who spoke at those meetings, so a recap may mention them. If you appear in a recap and would like it corrected or removed, contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## Children

The app is a general-audience civic information service. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

We hold almost nothing about you. If you sent a report and want it deleted, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the approximate date and what you reported. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over personal information; contact us and we will respond.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
