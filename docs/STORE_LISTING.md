## Description (both stores)
Akakū is Maui's community media center. This app puts it in your pocket.

WATCH LIVE
Channels 53, 54 and 55 stream live, with what's on now, what's next, and a progress bar for the current show.

LISTEN
KAKU 88.5 FM, "The Voice of Maui", plays in the app and keeps playing when your phone is locked.

FOLLOW THE ISSUES THAT MATTER
Start with the water issues and add the ones you care about. For each issue, see what has happened in Maui County meetings, in plain English. Every statement has a time stamp: tap it and you are at that moment in the recording, so you can check it yourself.

WATCH AKAKŪ'S VIDEOS
Akakū's own videos, newest first.

A NOTE ON AI
Recaps and issue pages are written by artificial intelligence from recordings of public meetings. AI can make mistakes, and recaps are not the official record. Spot something wrong? Tap "Report an error".

Free to use. Akakū is a nonprofit community media center and is not part of the County of Maui. To give or become a member, open the Support tab.

## Apple App Review notes (paste into the review notes field)
No sign-in is needed. The app streams Akakū Maui Community Media's own live television channels (53, 54, 55) and its radio station (KAKU 88.5 FM), shows Akakū's own YouTube videos, and publishes plain-English summaries of recorded public meetings of Maui County boards. The summaries are written by AI from public recordings and the app discloses this and offers "Report an error" on every statement. The app sends no personal data to any AI service; the AI runs on Akakū's own systems on public video. The Support tab's "Give or become a member" button opens the nonprofit's own donation page in a browser. Akakū owns or licenses all video and audio content shown.
