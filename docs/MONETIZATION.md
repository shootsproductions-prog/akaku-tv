# Monetization and launch plan (decided Oct 11, 2026)

## Principles
- Launch free, fully open, before the Nov 3 election. Learn who uses it, then decide on paying with real numbers.
- Charge for convenience and alerts, never for the facts. Civic information stays free.
- Every issue is approved by a person before it goes public. The AI drafts and re-checks; a human approves and follows up. There is no auto-publish by tag.

## Launch scope (Nov 3)
In: Live channels with now/next, KAKU 88.5 stream (schedule once staff confirm the weekly list), Videos, Meetings recaps, Issues (water plus whatever the producer approves), Support (give page), Report an error (email to start), "Notify me" email sign-up.
Out: accounts, payments, push notifications, candidate or ballot-measure content. Election content only from official sources, reviewed by a person first.
Review capacity sets the number of issues: cap the launch at what the producer can check (the 5 water issues plus a handful), add more as they are approved.
Main schedule risk: Apple enrollment approval plus app review. If Apple is not approved by about Oct 23, Android ships first.

## Who it is for
County workers following meetings; residents following a case that affects them; community organizers; lawyers; politicians and staff; planners and builders following a case. The professionals are the people with a reason to check often, and the likeliest to pay for alerts later.

## Revenue options (in order)
1. Donations and membership: the Support tab already links to https://www.akaku.org/give/.
2. Grants: local-news and civic-tech funders; launch numbers and election timing help the case.
3. Underwriting and sponsors: public-media model; needs editorial-independence rules; after the election.
4. "Pro" tier for professionals: alerts, saved searches, weekly digest, exports. Build only if the "Notify me" sign-ups show demand.

## Growth: Google Ad Grants (once /track is live)
Akakū has a Google Ad Grants credit (free search ads for nonprofits). Use it to point people at akaku.org/track, the web version of the tracker that can also be installed as an app from the home screen.
- Start only after /track is live, fast, and measured (GA4 events linked to the Ads account).
- Search ads only; neutral keywords about issues and meetings (Maui water, Bill 1, Lahaina permits, East Maui ditch, County Council meetings), never candidates or ballot measures.
- Check the current Ad Grants rules in the account (account manager, minimum click rate, conversion tracking, election-ad policy).
- Review after two weeks: clicks, top searches (may suggest new issues), installs, sign-ups. Details on the board: ask-website-google-ads-plan.

## What to measure in the first 60 days
Installs, weekly active users, issues followed per person, return visits, "Notify me" sign-ups, donation taps, error reports. The producer decides with the boss which of these is the headline.

## If a paid tier is built (after the election)
- Supabase (optional email sign-in, synced follows, notification queue), RevenueCat (Apple and Google in-app purchases; check current fees and nonprofit terms), Expo push.
- Accounts require: a rewritten privacy policy, new Apple and Google privacy forms, an in-app "delete my account" button, and email-only collection.
- Subscription covers: following more than the default issues, push alerts, weekly digest. Titles and summaries stay free.
